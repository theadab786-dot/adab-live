# 📱 Adab Live — Client Handover & Easy Website Management Guide

> **Welcome to your new website!**  
> This guide is specially written for you. **You do not need to know any programming or code** to manage your website. Everything can be updated directly from your visual **Owner Control Center** using your computer, tablet, or phone.

## 🔑 1. How to Log In to Your Control Center

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, etc.).
2. Go to your admin address:  
   👉 **`https://yourwebsite.com/admin`** (or `http://localhost:3000/admin` during development)
3. If not already signed in, you will see the **Band Owner Portal** login screen:
   - **Email:** `owner@adablive.com` *(or your custom email)*
   - **Password:** `SOHANA@2026` *(or your updated password)*
4. Click **Sign In to Dashboard**.

## 📸 3. How to Upload, Change & Delete Photos

You can manage photos everywhere on the website with simple point-and-click buttons:

### A. Band Lineup Photos (Solo, Duet, Full Band)
1. In your dashboard, click the **🎸 Live Band Lineups** tab.
2. Click on the band lineup you want to update (e.g. *Solo* or *Five-Piece Band*) to expand it.
3. Scroll down to the **Photo Gallery** section:
   - **To Upload a New Photo:** Click the purple **Upload New Photo** button. Select a picture from your computer or phone. It will upload instantly!
   - **To Make a Photo the Main Cover:** Hover over any photo thumbnail and click the **Star ⭐** icon. That photo will immediately become the primary card image.
   - **To Delete a Photo:** Hover over the photo thumbnail and click the red **Trash Can 🗑️** icon. The photo will be removed right away.
   - **To Preview in Full Screen:** Click the **Maximize 🔍** icon on any image to view it in full screen.
4. Click the purple **Save Changes** button. Your changes are immediately live on the website!

---

### B. Sound Equipment & Stage Audio Photos
1. In your dashboard, click the **🔊 Sound Setups & Specs** tab.
2. Click on any sound setup (e.g. *Basic Setup*, *Setup 2*, or *Classic Setup*) to expand it.
3. Scroll down to **Equipment Photos**:
   - Click **Upload New Photo** to add a new photo of your speakers, mixer, microphones, or live stage.
   - Click the red **Trash Can 🗑️** icon to delete old photos.
4. Click **Save All Sound**.

---

### C. Homepage & About Page Banner Photos
1. In your dashboard, click the **📞 Contact & About** tab.
2. Scroll to the **Key Page Cover Photos** section:
   - **Homepage Hero Cover Photo:** Click *Upload New Photo* to change the main background image on your front page.
   - **About Page Featured Photo:** Click *Upload New Photo* to change the showcase photo on the About Us page.
3. Click the purple **Save All Changes** button.

---

### D. Central Media Hub (Bulk Uploads)
1. Click the **🖼️ Media Hub** tab.
2. Click **Upload Photos** to upload multiple images at once from a camera or photographer's folder.
3. You can click the **Copy Link 📋** button on any photo to copy its URL.

## 📞 4. How to Change Contact Details & About Info

1. Click the **📞 Contact & About** tab.
2. Edit any of the following fields:
   - **Phone Number:** Displayed across the website for calls.
   - **WhatsApp Number:** Digits only (with country code, e.g. `919876543210` or `15559876543`). This powers the instant WhatsApp button.
   - **Official Email Address:** Where customer booking inquiries are sent.
   - **Base Location / Address:** Your city or studio address.
   - **About Story / Bio:** Your band's background story and promise.
   - **Social Links:** Paste your Instagram profile, YouTube channel, or Facebook page link.
   - **Video Clips:** Add YouTube performance links to show on your homepage video carousel.
3. Click **Save All Changes** at the top or bottom of the page.
4. Refresh your website — all headers, footers, and contact pages will display your new details immediately!

---

## 🎸 5. How to Change Band Lineups & Prices

1. Click the **🎸 Live Band Lineups & Photos** tab.
2. Click any lineup to open the editor:
   - Change the **Formation Name** (e.g. *Acoustic Solo*, *Trio*, *Big Band*).
   - Change the **Starting Fee (₹ INR)** (e.g. `15000` or `25000`).
   - Change the **Price Note** (e.g. *Starting price for 3-hour event*).
   - Edit **Band Members & Instruments:** Click **Add Musician** to add roles (e.g. *Saxophonist*, *Violinist*, *Drummer*) or click the trash icon to remove a role.
3. Want to add a whole new package? Click **+ Add New Lineup** at the top right!
4. Click **Save All Lineups**.

---

## 🔊 6. How to Change Sound Setups & Audio Specs

1. Click the **🔊 Sound Setups & Specs** tab.
2. Click any setup to open its specifications:
   - Update **Setup Name** and **Crowd Capacity** (e.g. *20 to 40+ people gathering* or *80 to 250 open area*).
   - Update **Base Price** and **Price Range Display Text** (e.g. `₹20,000 – ₹35,000`).
   - Update **Speakers, Monitors, Bass Subwoofers, Microphones, and Sound Operators**.
   - Add or edit bullet point features.
3. Click **Save All Sound**.

---

## 📅 7. How to Manage Bookings & Block Dates

### Managing Booking Inquiries
1. Click the **📅 Bookings** tab.
2. You will see all customer quote requests submitted through the website.
3. Click the **Eye 👁️** icon on any booking to view the customer's guest count, event type, date, venue address, and personal notes.
4. Click **Confirm** to accept the booking (this will automatically notify the customer) or **Decline** if you are unavailable.

### Blocking Dates on the Calendar
1. If you are already booked for a private event or going on holiday, click the **🚫 Blocked Dates** tab.
2. Choose the date from the date picker, type a brief reason (e.g. *Booked for private wedding*), and click **Add to Blocked Dates**.
3. That date will immediately become grayed out and unclickable in the booking calendar on your website!

## 💡 8. Pro Tips for High-Quality Photos

- **Recommended Formats:** JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WEBP.
- **File Size:** Under 5MB is ideal so that visitors on mobile phones can load your pages instantly without lag.
- **Landscape Photos:** Horizontal (wide) pictures look best for hero banners and stage showcases.
- **Lighting:** Stage lighting, sunset outdoor shots, and crisp performance photos make your brand look world-class!
